YMCA 3-minute step test

30 cm (12") step, metronome 96 bpm (up-up-down-down), 3 minutes. Sit straight away and count your pulse for one full minute.

Concept2 2000 m row

A performance test, not a health norm. There's no validated 2k-to-VO2 max equation, so this doesn't feed the longevity grading.

Resting heart rate

Each +10 bpm is linked to about 9% higher all-cause mortality (Zhang 2016 meta-analysis). Track the trend, not single days.

Heart rate recovery

The drop from peak HR after a hard effort. It only means something if the peak was near-maximal (≥85% of max HR).
