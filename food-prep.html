4. Left to Plan This Week

Auto-fill spreads your own meals across the week (keeping to the repeat limit, favouring meals whose protein/carb/fat mix suits your targets) and adds a snack or two. Then for each day it scales every meal's 🔗 ratio ingredients together and moves ↕ range ingredients within their limits until the day hits your calories and macros. 📌 fixed ones never change. Picked meals yourself? Hit ⚖️ Fit portions. The small box is the portion size; the Ready tab turns it all into grams.

How long a cooked portion stays good in the fridge, and whether it can be frozen. Only batch meals (more than one portion) use this: boxes eaten later than that go in the freezer, or get a warning if the meal doesn't freeze. Single portions are cooked fresh on the day.

Set how each ingredient behaves: 🔗 Ratio ingredients are locked together (100g oats : 300g milk) and scaled to fit your day — weights are for the whole batch. 📌 Fixed is the same every portion (1 banana). ↕ Range lets the planner pick between a min and max per portion (peanut butter 0–30g). Cooking a big pot of chilli? Add what goes in the pot as 🔗 and set how many portions it makes.
